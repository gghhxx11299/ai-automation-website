Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our AI automation services

AutoCallAI is an AI-powered automation platform that helps businesses automate phone calls, emails, and WhatsApp messages. Our advanced AI technology handles customer interactions, follow-ups, appointments, and support inquiries efficiently.

Our AI phone system makes and receives calls using natural-sounding voices. It can handle appointments, follow-ups, surveys, and customer inquiries. The system understands context, responds appropriately, and can escalate to human agents when needed.

Billing is based on the number of AI interactions per month. Each initiated conversation or response counts as an interaction. Our plans range from 1,000 interactions for the starter plan to 20,000 for enterprise, with custom options available for higher volumes.
